FuseOS

Your Android phone and your Mac, as one. Five minutes to set up.

1. Install on the Mac

  1. Drag FuseOS onto Applications in this window.
  2. Open Applications and double-click FuseOS.
  3. macOS says it can't check the app. That is expected: FuseOS is free and not yet notarized by Apple. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to FuseOS. You only do this once.
  4. Sign in, or create an account. Use the same account on your phone.

2. Allow these on the Mac

FuseOS asks for each one when you first use the feature that needs it. If you said no, switch it on in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

SettingWhyNeeded?
Local NetworkYour phone and Mac talk directly over your Wi-Fi. Without it nothing links.Yes
NotificationsFiles arriving, your phone's calls and messages, codes to copy.Yes
AccessibilityUse your phone as a trackpad and keyboard, unlock the Mac with your phone, and paste a code with one click.For those features
Screen & System Audio RecordingSidecar: your phone as a second display.For Sidecar
BluetoothLocks the Mac when your phone walks away, and wakes it when you unlock the phone beside it.For nearby lock

Optional, in FuseOS → Account: Start at login, so sync is ready before you need it.

3. Install on the Android phone

  1. On the phone, go to fuseos.theshaik.dev/download and download the Android app.
  2. Open the APK. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Sign in with the same account as on the Mac.

4. Allow these on the phone

FuseOS → You lists each one with a Set up button that takes you to the right page.

SettingWhy
NotificationsShows files arriving and keeps FuseOS connected in the background.
Notification accessYour phone's notifications on the Mac, with Reply, and codes ready to paste.
Accessibility → FuseOSThe copy pop-up (copy anything, send it to the Mac), and letting the Mac tap and type while you mirror the screen.
Display over other appsThe island that offers each copy, over whatever app you're in.
Phone callsAnswer and decline calls from the Mac.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth)Lets the Mac tell how close your phone is, for nearby lock.
Battery → UnrestrictedSome phones freeze apps with the screen off. This keeps the link up.
Screen captureAsked each time you start mirroring the phone on the Mac.

5. Link them

Put both on the same Wi-Fi, signed in to the same account. They find each other in a few seconds. If not, tap Connect now on the phone, or Connect on the Mac.

Your privacy

What you copy, your files, notifications, screen and calls go straight between your own devices over your Wi-Fi, encrypted. They never reach our servers. Details: fuseos.theshaik.dev/privacy.
